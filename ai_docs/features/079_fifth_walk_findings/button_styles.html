How the pick lands: the letter becomes the ghost_button tier in one place (ui_primitives.py), so every low-emphasis site follows; the Reset variant becomes danger_button; the chip is chip_button on the caption row. Hover and active states darken by one step of the palette as the tiers do today. Colors are the theme's tokens, so the accent swap keeps working. Mixing is fine: e.g. "B for buttons, 3 for Reset, accent chip".
