094 · where the chips go

What is left to place after the settled cuts. Target fps, throttle on/off and GPU budget stay in Settings — they are global. Render all documents is deleted. So the canvas carries three things: the fps pill (with the profiler unfolding from it), the resolution picker, and the channel view. The script play/stop goes on the graph canvas. Options A–C place the canvas chips; D–F place the script control.
