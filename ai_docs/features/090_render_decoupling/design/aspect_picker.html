Aspect picker × resolution selector — four layouts

The Document tab's size controls today: a caption row (Document name · Canvas), the name field on the left, and on the right W × H integer fields plus a presets menu (squares, the video shapes, bound texture sizes). Revision 1 adds the mode: Auto stores an aspect only and renders at the viewer's size; Fixed stores W × H as today. Each variant is drawn in both states. The dashed 1214×683 style marks a read-only live number.

AMode toggle, the row swaps its content

The smallest change from today. A two-way segmented control replaces the current Fixed toggle; the same slot on the row shows aspect chips under Auto and the W × H fields plus presets under Fixed. The live size is a dim readout so the user always sees what the shader gets.

BOne menu owns both: aspects and resolutions

No explicit mode control. A single dropdown lists Aspect entries and Resolution entries in two groups; picking an aspect makes the document Auto, picking a resolution makes it Fixed. The W × H fields appear only after a resolution is picked (or "Custom…"). Fewest pixels; the mode is implied by what you chose.

CThe fields stay, locked under Auto and showing the live size

The W × H fields never leave. Under Auto they are read-only and show the live size as it changes with the viewer, with an aspect combo beside them; a lock glyph says why they are grey. Fixed unlocks them and the aspect combo disappears. Communicates "auto = derived from the canvas" the most directly.

DAspect in the caption, mode on the row, one combo
