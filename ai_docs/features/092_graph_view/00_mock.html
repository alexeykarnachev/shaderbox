Local mock, nothing here is wired. One layout engine (rank = longest path from a root, group members adjacent inside a rank, ports fanned per sampler) renders every variant, so what differs between panels is only the drawing. Two real documents: the six-pass Radiance Cascades example (a chain six ranks deep, two long edges from paint, two feedback passes, run counts 12 and 6) and the Bloom Chain fixture imported as group bloom into a host with scene, a stale vignette, and final. Hover a node to light its edges. Panels resize from the bottom-right corner; the slider sets them all.

1 · Ranks left to right, the strip's own tile the 091 teaser, honest about width

Node = the strip tile at 168 (thumb 112, name under it). Edges leave the source's right edge and enter the consumer's left edge at one port per sampler, the sampler's name at the head. An edge spanning more than one rank bows under the row so it does not cross the nodes between. Feedback is the loop on the top-right corner. Without fit this is the natural size; with fit the whole picture scales into the panel and the names shrink with it.

2 · Same, compact node 108 wide, 80 thumb

The same layout with a node that is a picture and a name and nothing else. The question is whether the picture still reads at 80 and at 80 times the fit zoom.

3 · Ranks top to bottom depth becomes height, which the panel has

The chain runs down instead of across. The Document tab is narrower than it is tall and it scrolls, so a deep chain costs scrolling rather than zoom. Long edges bow to the right.

4 · Lanes the git-log shape 070 liked, with the tiles

One column of nodes in strip order, every edge a lane on the left: it leaves the source's left edge, drops down its lane, and turns into the consumer's port with the sampler's name on the turn. Nothing crosses a node, nothing bows, the width is the tile plus the lane gutter, so it fits at 420 without zoom. Feedback stays a corner loop.

5 · Port-list nodes the sampler names live on the node, edges end at a dot

A node lists its samplers as input ports under the picture; the edge ends at the port's dot and carries no label of its own. Longer nodes, but every name has a fixed home and two edges into one node cannot collide. Left to right, compact.

6 · Orthogonal edges variant 2 with elbows instead of beziers

Same as 2, edges as rounded elbows routed through the gap between ranks. Reads more like a circuit, less like a drawing; the long edges take the gutter under the row.
